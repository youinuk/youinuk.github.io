About me.

Physics Teacher · Independent Researcher

BACKGROUND

I am a physics teacher based in Seoul and an independent researcher in quantum computing and quantum information.

My research interests span quantum computing and quantum information, with a particular focus on quantum hardware, silicon quantum dots, and semiconductor spin qubits. My background in physics, computer engineering, and Linux systems development informs this work.

Alongside this work, I develop executable notes and interactive resources for teaching quantum information, and share seminar and study-group presentations.
