About me.

Physics Teacher · Independent Researcher

BACKGROUND

I am a physics teacher based in Seoul and an independent researcher in silicon spin-qubit physics.

With a background in physics, computer engineering, and Linux systems development, I use scientific computing to explore questions in quantum hardware. My current research examines how modelling assumptions affect conclusions about spin–valley shuttling.

Alongside this work, I develop executable notes and interactive resources for teaching quantum information, and share presentations from my own learning and research.
