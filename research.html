Current work / Spin–valley shuttling

How much do design conclusions depend on the model?

My current study compares the sensitivity to an assumed local spin–valley coupling profile with the finite-sampling uncertainty of the calculation itself.

The work combines a four-level spin–valley model, micromagnet fields, and dephasing calculations, with code and analysis released for reproducibility.
