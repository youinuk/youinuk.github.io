My research interests span quantum computing and quantum information, with a particular focus on quantum hardware, silicon quantum dots, and semiconductor spin qubits.

Research interests

I am interested in how quantum information can be encoded, controlled, and transferred using electron spins in semiconductor devices.

Quantum computing & quantum information

The physical foundations of quantum computation and information, including quantum states, control, and quantum error correction.

Quantum hardware

Silicon quantum dots (Si QDs) and semiconductor spin qubits are my main focus within this broader field.

Scientific computing

I use numerical modelling and open code to investigate specific questions in quantum hardware and make calculations reproducible.

Model-form versus finite-sampling uncertainty in micromagnet-assisted spin–valley shuttling: a periodic-reference sensitivity atlas

INUK YOU · Single author
Submitted to Journal of Applied Physics.

This study examines one question in spin–valley shuttling: how sensitivity to an assumed local coupling profile compares with finite-sampling uncertainty.

It combines a four-level spin–valley model, micromagnet fields, and dephasing calculations. Code, data, and analysis are available below.
