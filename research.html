Research interests

I am interested in how quantum information can be encoded, controlled, and transferred using electron spins in semiconductor devices.

Quantum computing & quantum information

The physical foundations of quantum computation and information, including quantum states, control, and quantum error correction.

Quantum hardware

Silicon quantum dots (Si QDs) and semiconductor spin qubits are my main focus within this broader field.

Scientific computing

I use simulation and open code to study assumptions in spin-qubit control and calibration. I am particularly interested in the measurements needed to connect these models to real devices; numerical reproducibility alone does not establish physical validity.

Spin–valley shuttling response study

INUK YOU · Independent computational research

This study compares how four diagnostic coupling ansätze (profiles) change calculated responses within one phenomenological four-level spin–valley model and a controlled-reference periodic micromagnet geometry. The ansätze were not microscopically derived.

The results are conditional on those assumptions and are not validated device-performance predictions. The model’s physical interpretation is under reassessment.

Archived manuscript: Model-form versus finite-sampling uncertainty in micromagnet-assisted spin–valley shuttling: a periodic-reference sensitivity atlas. The archived release preserves the original numerical study; it contains no revised Hamiltonian, solver, or corresponding numerical results.
